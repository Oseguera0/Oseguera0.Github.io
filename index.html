Weekly Mock Contests

Each week, moderators post a Mock Contest Challenge — fun, low-stakes prompts like “Tell a story in 15 seconds” or “Your most suspenseful scene.” Creators submit entries by linking videos hosted on YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram. Fans vote, debate, and help decide the winners.

Mock contests serve two purposes: they give creators practice in a competitive environment, and they give fans a taste of what it’s like to follow and support their favorites. These are the training grounds for the main GenerationOne arena.

Forum Hall of Fame

At the end of each mock contest, winners are announced and added to the Hall of Fame. These threads preserve the history of our earliest champions and ensure their names are remembered when the full platform launches.

Monthly Mock Festival Week

Once a month, winners of previous contests face off in a bigger, fan-driven showdown. Fans can cast votes, debate, and cheer for their champions in a final community poll. The winner earns the special title of Festival Champion.

Recognition & Rewards

Prizes at this stage are not monetary. Creators earn badges, profile titles, and recognition — rewards that matter because early champions will carry their achievements into the full GenerationOne launch. Your forum victories today help shape your reputation forever.

Rules & Moderation

We follow strict regulations and rules to keep the community safe and thriving. Every post, comment, and submission must comply with community standards. Offensive, harmful, or illegal content will be automatically flagged, removed, and investigated.

3‑Strike Policy

First Strike
Warning and removal of the violating content.
Second Strike
Temporary suspension from posting.
Third Strike
Permanent account ban from the GenerationOne ecosystem.

Hybrid Moderation

Our automated system immediately flags content that violates rules (e.g., hate speech, spam, or inappropriate material). Human moderators then review flagged posts to confirm decisions. This hybrid approach keeps the community fair and safe.

Constructive Culture

The GenerationOne Forum thrives on constructive engagement. Criticism is welcome, but it must be respectful and aimed at helping others improve. Today’s beginners could be tomorrow’s legends — and you may be watching them rise right here.

Fans Are Kingmakers

Fans have just as much power here as creators. By voting, sharing theories, and fueling discussions, fans shape the narrative. Creators compete — but fans amplify voices and help decide who becomes great.

From Forum to Forever

While the forum is the prelaunch hub, it is also a permanent feature of GenerationOne. When the full platform launches, this forum will evolve into the Community Tab of GenerationOne itself. Your account, your posts, and your achievements will carry over seamlessly.

Nothing here is temporary. Your posts, contest victories, theories, and friendships will follow you into the main site. GenerationOne remembers its founders.
